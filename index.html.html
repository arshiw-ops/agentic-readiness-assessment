WHAT IS THIS ASSESSMENT?

As AI systems evolve from passive tools into active agents, we need to ask: "Is this system governed, ethical, safe, and accountable?"

This Agentic Readiness Assessment helps cross-functional teams evaluate whether AI systems are ready for autonomous operation across four critical lenses: Governance, Ethics, Safety, and Responsibility (GESR).

HOW TO USE THIS ASSESSMENT

  1. Identify the AI system to assess
  2. Assemble cross-functional team (90-minute session):
    • AI/ML technical teams
    • Security representatives
    • Business owners
    • Legal/Compliance
    • Ethics or Responsible AI functions
  3. Work through each lens tab
  4. Review Summary Dashboard
  5. Use Action Planning tab

SCORING GUIDE

  • YES (2 points): Fully in place
  • PARTIAL (1 point): Incomplete
  • NO (0 points): Does not exist

Max per lens: 16 points | Total max: 64 points

MATURITY LEVELS

0-21: CRITICAL - Not Ready
22-42: CAUTION - Partially Ready
43-64: READY - Mature
⚠️ Important: Your responses are not automatically saved. Complete the assessment in one session, or use the Print button on Dashboard to save your progress.
